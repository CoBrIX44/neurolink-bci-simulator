ENGINEERING FOUNDATION

Why 1–40 Hz Zero-Phase Filtering?

Scalp potentials are distorted by two primary non-neural contaminants:

Low-Frequency Drift (<1 Hz): Galvanic skin impedance changes and sweating cause massive baseline wander.
High-Frequency Artifacts (>40 Hz): Jaw clenching muscle potentials (EMG) and 50/60 Hz electrical mains noise.
Zero-Phase filtfilt: Filters forward and backward. Eliminates phase shifts so peaks remain locked at their true physiological timing.
