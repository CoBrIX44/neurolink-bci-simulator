## LinkedIn / Professional
Introducing NeuroLink: an educational laboratory for Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) simulation.

BCI literature often jumps straight from EEG sensors to robotic actuators, skipping the mathematical bridge that makes digital control possible. NeuroLink makes that bridge visible and interactive:

1. Synthesis: Generates 256 Hz synthetic electrophysiology with controlled pink noise, 60 Hz mains interference, and baseline drift.
2. Digital Filtering: Implements 4th-order forward/backward Butterworth band-pass (1–40 Hz) for zero-phase distortion.
3. Feature Extraction: Computes discrete Fourier transform power across discrete frequency bands ([1, 12) Hz vs [12, 40] Hz).
4. Linear Classification: Evaluates standardized logistic regression and demonstrates simulated linear cursor deflection.

Built with Python 3.12, NumPy, SciPy, and Streamlit, wrapped in the Google Stitch 'Editorial Scientific Laboratory' design language.
